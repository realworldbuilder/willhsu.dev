The Deadmines

Everything to do before you zone in: the seven quests, where they start, and the chains behind them. Alliance quests; Horde can run it, but gets none of them. The run itself (bosses and loot) is next.

Pre-reqs: the short version

  • Be 17 or higher. The group finder lists it for 17 to 26. Alba Fairmoon's dungeon step is level 18.
  • Start Alba Fairmoon's chain early. It's 11 quests and the dungeon is step 10. Do steps 1 to 9 in Westfall first or you'll zone in without it.
  • Pick up three quests in Stormwind: two from Wilder Thistlenettle, one from Shoni the Shilent.
  • Pick up two at Sentinel Hill: Red Silk Bandanas, and The Defias Brotherhood if you've done its legwork (it sends you to Lakeshire first).
  • Paladins: The Test of Righteousness needs lumber from inside.
  • Set your hearthstone at Sentinel Hill and grab the flight path there. Most turn-ins are in Westfall.

The new one: Toxic Soil

Forever added this chain on 4 November. Every step comes from Alba Fairmoon in Westfall. Her first quest points you to the Jansen Stead "in the north" and the Molsen Farm "northwest of here", which puts her at Sentinel Hill, around the inn. spot not confirmed

Destruction in Deadminesnew in foreverAlba Fairmoon · Westfall · level 18

Find the forge hidden in the Deadmines and plant her Extra-Destructive Explosives by it. Then meet her at the Deadmines exit, in the hills behind Moonbrook, and use the detonator (a second quest with the same name).

Sentinel Hill, Westfall

The Defias Brotherhoodfrom classic · check in gameGryan Stoutmantle · Sentinel Hill

The last step of a long chain: bring back VanCleef's head. Good rewards when you hand it in.

Pre-req: the earlier steps. Gryan sends you to Wiley the Black in Lakeshire (Redridge), then back to Westfall to deal with a Defias messenger. If you have the Lakeshire flight path, fly; otherwise it is a long run east through Elwynn.

Red Silk BandanasScout Riell · Sentinel Hill tower

10 Red Silk Bandanas off the Defias inside.

Not to be confused with The People's Militia, Gryan's other chain. That one is killing Defias around Westfall and has nothing to do with the dungeon.

Inside

The Unsent Letterfrom classic · check in gameDrops off Edwin VanCleef

Take it to Baros Alexston in Stormwind City Hall, Cathedral Square. It leads on to a short chain in Stormwind.

Class quest

The Test of Righteousnessfrom classic · check in gamePaladins · Jordan Stilwell, outside Ironforge

Part of the Verigan's Fist chain. You need Whitestone Oak Lumber from the Goblin Woodcarvers in the Deadmines.

Getting there

The Deadmines is under Moonbrook, the ruined town in southern Westfall. Ride from Sentinel Hill, go into the mine in Moonbrook, and follow it down past the undead miners (your union cards and the Foreman) to the instance portal. Summon or meet the group there.
