Rotation

Midnight removed Bloodtalons and Brutal Slash. It's cleaner now.

The opener, every pull

Single target

  1. On cooldown. 50 energy back and +15% damage.
  2. At 5 if missing or under ~30% left. Never clip it.
  3. At 5 combo points with 50+ energy and Rip safe.
  4. If missing or about to fall off.
  5. Build combo points. Clearcasting makes it free.

AoE (3+ mobs)

  1. Rake 2–3 targets first.
  2. At 5 to Rip everything at once.
  3. Build combo points. Spend Clearcasting here.
  4. Optional filler. Guides disagree on its value in Cat.
  5. Still on cooldown, AoE or not.

Expert habits

What separates a good Feral from a great one.

Open from Prowl

Stealth Rake hits harder, and with Pouncing Strikes it stuns too. Prowl is back in about 6 seconds between pulls.

Tiger's Fury on cooldown

It's free energy and damage. Later, line up Berserk and Convoke the Spirits with it.

Finish at 5 points

Spending fewer combo points is a DPS loss. Watch the pips, not the energy bar.

Don't clip Rip

Refresh it only in the last ~30% of its duration. Earlier wastes the leftover ticks.

Bear is your panic button

Shift-2 into Bear, then Shift-T Frenzied Regeneration and Shift-4 Barkskin. Shift-1 back to Cat.

Ditch WeakAuras

It's dead in Midnight. Use Blizzard's built-in Cooldown Manager in Edit Mode instead.
