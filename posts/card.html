WristAssist

Voice notes from your wrist, transcribed entirely on-device with WhisperKit. No cloud, no account.

Use: Tap your wrist, talk, read it back.

"Never leaves your phone."

Watch → iPhone · on-device

OBS Image Reaction

An OBS source that shows one image while you are silent and another while you speak, with movement effects and blinking.

Use: Plug it in, add the feed to OBS.

Close the lid

Say what I want, shut the laptop and walk off while a Mac mini in the corner does the work.

Rough edges

  • Nothing restarts a crashed bridge. The LaunchAgent runs once at login.
  • The mini needs its own credentials. That's where the commands run.
  • Apps that need a screen need Screen Sharing.
