Start here: the ten minute setup

You just boosted. The game drops you in a Catch Up tutorial with a default bar and no hero tree picked. Do these in order, then skip the tutorial.

1 · Spec

Press N. Specialization tab: Marksmanship. Then the Talents tab.

2 · Import the build

Bottom left of the talent panel, click the loadout dropdown ("Default Loadout"), choose Import, paste the string below, give it a name, Import. That sets the class tree, the spec tree and the Dark Ranger hero tree in one go. It includes Avian Specialization, so you play without a pet; do not panic when it vanishes.

Open world and dungeons: the string above (Method's Dark Ranger AoE / Mythic+ build, 12.1.5). Raid bosses: C4PAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAYzsMwAmgZYbAzCAAAAAAAAYGzYGmxMzYGMmmxgx2yMzMzMzMzswMLDGAAg5BmZGzMzACDDwGzsNjB

3 · Turn on the bars

Escape, Options, Action Bars: tick Action Bars 2, 3 and 5. Bar 2 is Shift, bar 3 is Ctrl, bar 5 sits on the right for clicking. Lock Action Bars stays ticked.

4 · Fill them like this page

Drag from the spellbook (P) onto the slots below. Mounts come from Shift-P, the Summon Random Favorite Mount button is at the top right of the journal. Hearthstone and potions drag straight out of the bag.

5 · Leave Catch Up

Red X on "Leave Catch Up" at the top right of the screen. You land in Dornogal with the Midnight campaign in your quest log. Follow the Campaign tracker on the right ("Champions of Quel'Danas" and on); that is the 80 to 90 road.

6 · Set your hearth

Talk to the innkeeper in Dornogal (or the first Midnight hub) so Ctrl-6 brings you home. Star two mounts in the journal so Ctrl-1 has something to pick from.

Keybinds

Same keys as Scarletfire: 1 to 5 and Q E R F G T Z. Every fight button is on the main bar. Shift is the bar above it (escapes, trap, stuns, Misdirection). Ctrl is the bar above that (mounts, hearth, eagle). The vertical bar on the right is click-only: potion, food, bandage.

Rotation

One idea: Aimed Shot and Rapid Fire make Precise Shots, Black Arrow spends them. Everything else is a cooldown you press when it is ready.

Talents

Method's Dark Ranger AoE / Mythic+ build (12.1.5), imported as the loadout "Dark Ranger AoE Method". The cards are the talents that change how you play.

Where Chinacat went

Not a bug and not something you did. Your talent build has Avian Specialization, which trades the pet for an eagle. Call Pet does nothing while it is on.

The talent

Avian Specialization (Marksmanship tree) removes your ability to use a pet and gives you a non-interactive eagle instead. The eagle is what Fetch: Eagle and Harrier's Cry come from, and it buffs Aimed Shot and Rapid Fire. Blizzard's default level 80 loadout takes it, and so do most guide builds.

Want Chinacat back?

Open talents (N), search "Avian", click the node to refund it, spend the point elsewhere, Apply. Call Pet works again and Chinacat comes out of the stable. You lose the eagle buffs, about a few percent of damage, and gain a wolf that holds mobs off you. For solo leveling that is a fair trade; for dungeons the eagle wins.

Why the pet was out earlier

The Catch Up scenario summoned him before the loadout finished applying. Once it did, he was dismissed and the Pet tab left your spellbook.

What is on the bars now

The pet buttons are gone. Shift-Z is Wing Clip, Ctrl-7 is Fetch: Eagle (the eagle loots corpses for you), Ctrl-8 is Flare, and Ctrl-9 onwards is empty.

Stats, gear and everything else

The boost set is item level 102. Quest rewards pass it within an evening, so do not spend gold on gear.

Stat priority

Agility first, then Critical Strike, Mastery, Versatility, Haste (wow.gg, 12.1.5). When two quest rewards tie on item level, take the one with Crit or Mastery. Ranged weapon upgrades beat everything.

Gear while leveling

Take every quest reward that raises item level. Dungeons through the Group Finder (I) drop better. At 90, crafted and dungeon gear start to matter; not before.

Consumables

The boost gave healing potions, food and bandages; they are on the right bar. Eat out of combat below half health, potion in combat, bandage when neither is up.

The windows

C character, P spellbook, N talents, Shift-P mounts and pets, B bags, M map, L quest log, I group finder. Escape then Edit Mode moves any frame; the Cooldown Manager in there replaces WeakAuras.

Talents as you level

Every level to 90 gives points. Open N when it glows: hero points into the two empty Dark Ranger nodes first, then follow the gold highlights.

Sentinel instead?

The other hero tree. Guides rate it a little higher in dungeons and raids right now. One click in the talent panel swaps; the bars do not change except Black Arrow becomes Kill Shot on Q.

Turn on Assisted Highlight

Escape, Options, Gameplay Enhancements, tick Assisted Highlight. The game then glows the button it thinks you should press next, using this exact priority. It only works for abilities that are on a bar, which is why everything is on one. Do not use the Single-Button Assistant spell: it adds a global cooldown penalty.

Interrupt nag

The game keeps saying "add an interrupt ability". Counter Shot is already on R; the nag stops once you use it in a fight.

Expert habits

What a good Marksman does without thinking.

Open from range

Mark, then start Aimed Shot before the mob notices you. It lands as the pull begins and you already have Precise Shots.

Black Arrow first

If it is lit, it beats everything. Above 80% and below 20% it is always lit; in between it needs a Deathblow proc, which Rapid Fire guarantees.

Never cap Aimed Shot

Two charges sitting there is wasted damage. Watch the little counter on key 1.

Cooldowns are not rare

Explosive Shot, Volley and Rapid Fire come back every 16 to 45 seconds. Press them every pull, do not save them.

Kite, do not tank

Binding Shot at your feet, Disengage away (Dark Chains slows the nearest one), keep shooting. Exhilaration under 50%, Turtle when it goes wrong, Feign Death to reset.

Ditch WeakAuras

It is dead in Midnight. Use Blizzard's built-in Cooldown Manager in Edit Mode instead; it tracks Precise Shots and Deathblow for you.
