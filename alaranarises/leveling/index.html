Finding the door

The boost drops you in Dornogal with a quest log full of The War Within. None of it is Midnight. Here is what to ignore and where the real start is.

Ignore "Something on the Horizon"

Brann Bronzebeard's quest is The War Within's level-up campaign skip. Same for Surface Bound, A Time to Come Together, The Fleet Arrives and the "Meet Arator" lore chain by the bank. They do nothing for Midnight.

The quest is called "Midnight"

It is auto-accepted when you own the expansion: "Locate the nearest Image of Lady Liadrin". If it is not in your log, open the Adventure Guide (Shift-J) › Suggested Content.

Image of Lady Liadrin

Dornogal, Keepers Terrace at 44.2, 34.8. Also Orgrimmar, Valley of Strength at 53.4, 77.3 by the Broken Tusk. Talk to her and you are teleported to the Isle of Quel'Danas.

The intro scenario

Defend the Sunwell, then retake Silvermoon: seventeen quests, 15 to 20 minutes on Quel'Danas plus the Silvermoon half, about an hour all told. Mounting is off during the Sunwell part. It ends in Silvermoon with "Paved in Ash". This is the first Midnight character on the account, so there is no skip.

Set your hearth in Silvermoon

Wayfarer's Rest, the inn. Ctrl-6 then brings you back to the hub for the whole expansion.

Fly

Skyriding and steady flight work in every Midnight zone from the moment the scenario ends; the Pathfinder gate was removed in March 2026. If a boosted character cannot mount after the intro, summon the Highland Drake once (Ctrl-2) and everything works after.

The road: 80 to 90

Four zones, seventeen campaign chapters, about 3.7 million XP. The campaign alone gets you to 90 or within a world quest of it. Ten hours at a normal pace, five to seven if you skip every side quest.

Side quests

Only the ones that overlap a campaign objective. The campaign plus the first run of each dungeon and the first Delve in each zone is enough XP.

Delves while leveling

Patch 12.0.7 raised first-time Delve XP a lot. The fastest known route is Delve-heavy. One Delve per zone the first time you pass it is free XP and starts Valeera's levels.

Prey hunts and renown weeklies

Also buffed in 12.0.7. Take them when they are next to you, do not travel for them.

Exploration XP is gone

12.1 cut it by 60%. Flying around uncovering the map no longer levels you.

Leveling as a Marksman

The spec was built for standing still, and leveling is all movement. These are the adjustments.

Pull two or three

Hunter's Mark the one that will live longest, Aimed Shot the second, Explosive Shot on two of them, Volley, then Black Arrow whenever it lights. Three mobs die about as fast as one.

Open from maximum range

Aimed Shot is a 2.5 second cast. Start it before the mob notices; it lands as the fight starts and you already have Precise Shots.

Move on instants

Arcane Shot, Black Arrow, Explosive Shot, Multi-Shot and Volley all cast while moving. Rapid Fire channels while moving. Aimed Shot and Steady Shot do not, so stand for those two.

Binding Shot at your feet

Shift-R. Anything that reaches you is stunned a moment later, and Disengage (Shift-2) puts you back at range with Dark Chains slowing the nearest one.

Exhilaration is a heal

T, under half health, no cost. Use it every fight that needs it; it is back in two minutes.

The pet question

Row three of the spec tree is a choice: Avian Specialization (the eagle, what this character runs) or Unbreakable Bond (a real pet). A tenacity pet like Chinacat holds mobs off you and costs about 3% damage. For solo leveling that is a fair trade; for dungeons and 90 the eagle wins. Swap in the talent panel, no re-taming needed, the stable is still there.

Dark Ranger or Sentinel for leveling

Nobody has measured it for questing. Dark Ranger's Bleak Powder cone suits the multi-mob pulls above; Sentinel is a touch stronger on a single target. Keep Dark Ranger until 90, decide then with the rotation page.

Spend every talent point

N glows at each level: the two empty Dark Ranger nodes first, then the gold highlights. The Apex talents at the end of the spec tree open as you level; Marksmanship's is the Take Aim line.

Dungeons while leveling

Group Finder (I), queue as damage, Misdirection on the tank (Shift-Q) before every pull. One run of each Midnight dungeon with its campaign quest is the whole plan; after that the quest XP beats the queue time.

The moment you hit 90

The first hour at cap, in order. After this the gear page takes over.

  1. "The Last Push" from Lor'themar Theron in SilvermoonTurns on world quests and the rest of the endgame.
  2. "Midnight: World Tour" and "Feeding the Flame"One of each weekly event. World Tour pays the first Spark of Tides.
  3. "Midnight Delves!" and Valeera SanguinarDelver's Headquarters, Silvermoon 52.5, 78.3. Then "The Season Starts: Midnight".
  4. Two Apex Cache weeklies outside the bankVeteran gear for showing up.
  5. Field Accolades to Maren Silverwing200 for an Adventurer cache, 500 for a random Veteran piece, 750 for a chosen slot.
  6. ProfessionsTrainers just north of the Sanctum of Light: Talmar (Leatherworking) 43.2, 55.8 and Tyn (Skinning) 43.3, 55.6, or Danwe (Engineering) 43.6, 54.1.
  7. Heroic dungeons at 266, the Lair at 273, Delves to tier 8The ladder is on the gear page.
