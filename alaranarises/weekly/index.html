Every day

Short list on purpose.

Every session and every pull

Not ticked, just read until they are habits.

Before a key

Flask, food, rune, pot in the bag, Misdirection on Shift-Q, the hero tree you mean to run, a quest log under 20.

Every pull

Hunter's Mark on the big one. Misdirection on the tank. Aimed Shot started before the tank is in range. Explosive Shot on two targets. Black Arrow whenever it lights up.

Every fight that goes wrong

Under 50%: Exhilaration (T). Big hit coming: Survival of the Fittest (Shift-4). Melee on you: Binding Shot at your feet (Shift-R), Disengage (Shift-2). Dying: Turtle (Z), then Feign Death (Shift-3) when it ends.

Every level to 90

N glows: spend the point. Hero tree empties first. Equip any quest reward with a higher item level; Crit or Mastery breaks ties. Spellbook (P) for anything new.

Every patch

Re-import the talent string from Method or Icy Veins. Walk the bars: a changed talent leaves a greyed button. Update these pages.
