At 80: what the boost gave you and what it buys

Checked in the bags on 3 Nov 2026. The short version: nothing for sale at level 80 beats the boost set, because every piece you can buy was squished to the same place.

So how do you gear at 80? You do not. Item level 102 is tuned for Eversong Woods, quest rewards in the first zone already pass it, and by 83 the whole boost set is gone. Every hour spent shopping in Dornogal is an hour not spent on the road to 90, where the list below starts paying.

Stats

Every hand-written 12.1.5 guide agrees on the shape. Item level first, because Agility beats any secondary.

Weapon damage, then Agility

A ranged weapon upgrade beats everything else in the bag. After that, higher item level is more Agility and wins almost every time.

Critical Strike

Feeds Penetrating Shots, Lethality, Critical Precision and the capstones. The Bell Tolls adds 3% on top. Your best secondary.

Mastery

Sniper Training: more range and more damage on everything. Second.

Versatility

Flat damage and flat damage reduction. Third, and the one to like in keys.

Haste

Only speeds Auto Shot, Focus and Aimed Shot. Last, no breakpoints to chase.

Tertiaries

Method says Avoidance, then Speed, then Leech. Top key players wear Leech first. Either way, never pick a tertiary over item level.

The order is Agility > Crit > Mastery > Versatility > Haste from Method, Icy Veins and Murlok. Noxxic's auto-generated page puts Versatility and Haste first; ignore it. Nobody publishes a separate Dark Ranger order, so use this one for both hero trees.

Gems

One Eversong Diamond in your single unique socket: Powerful (most players) or Indecipherable. Every other socket a Flawless Masterful Garnet (Crit and Mastery). For the diamond's colour bonus, one each of Deadly Amethyst, Deadly Peridot and Deadly Lapis, then Garnets.

Buying sockets

Radiant Jewelbinder adds a socket to helm, bracers or belt, from Vaultkeeper Elysa in Silvermoon for 6 Thalassian Tokens of Merit. The season 2 version is the Miasmic Jewelbinder from the Great Vault vendor. Head, wrist and waist only; one socket per piece.

Hunter ammo and scopes

Engineering makes Laced Zoomshots and Weighted Boomshots (ammo) and Smuggler's Lynxeye and Farstrider's Hawkeye (scopes). Method's consumables list says these replace oils and weapon enchants for hunters, but every spec guide still lists Phoenix Oil plus a normal enchant. Test in game: if the oil buff drops when you load ammo, they do not stack.

Crafted gear and embellishments

Two embellished items at once, no more. Crafted gear tops out at 331, so it is only worth wearing for those two slots.

Spark of Tides

One a week from the weekly quests: Trailing Xal'atath (Vereesa Windrunner, Silvermoon, 100 Fading Voidwhispers), Sparks of War (Zerella, Falconwing Square, PvP) or Turn Back the Surge (Talon Commander Zela, Tokka's Landing, three Curse Surges). Armour costs two Sparks.

Crafted item level

Spark only: 305. Add 80 Hero crests: 318. Add 80 Myth crests: 331.

The two pieces

Farstrider's Trophy Belt and Farstrider's Plated Bracers, Leatherworking mail, Crit and Mastery, each with the Arcanoweave Lining embellishment (a mana wyrm that grants primary stat to you and an ally). 97 of 100 top-player embellishment slots are Arcanoweave.

Do not craft a weapon

Nebulous Voidcore bonus rolls can hand you a Myth-track weapon from a boss. A crafted one would be dead gold.

New season 2 embellishments

Adorned Fang, Snakeskin Lining, Polished Ammolite, Hunter's Ritual Stone, Coiled Snake-Eye. None of the hunter guides rate them over Arcanoweave; skip unless a sim says otherwise.

Skulking Viper's Ambush, the hunter tier

Two pieces: Explosive Shot lasts one second longer and does 5% more. Four pieces: Aimed Shot and Rapid Fire do 5% more, and every Explosive Shot burst takes half a second off both cooldowns. Explosive Shot on cooldown is already rule two of the rotation; the set is why.

Where the pieces drop

Head from The Twin Fangs, hands from Entombed Sentinels, chest from Vashnik the Malignant, shoulders from The Lost Explorers, legs from Sszorak. Ula'tek drops the Slumbering Coil Curio, a token Kirana in Silvermoon turns into any slot.

The Catalyst

In the Silvermoon Bazaar next to the Voidforge. Venomblight Manaflux charges: one every two weeks, cap eight, plus extra from Serpent Scion and (after your first set bonus) from keys, raid bosses and Bountiful delves. Converted pieces keep their secondaries.

Professions

Pick two in Silvermoon. The class answer and the gold answer are different.

Leatherworking + Skinning

The class pick. Leatherworking crafts the belt and bracers above and the Adorned Fang embellishment; Skinning feeds it Void-Tempered Leather and sells the rest.

Gold instead

Inscription (missives on every crafted piece), Jewelcrafting and Alchemy top every 2026 gold list. Herbalism or Mining if you want zero crafting-order effort.

Engineering

Only if you want the hunter ammo and scopes above. Check that they stack with oil and a weapon enchant before committing.
