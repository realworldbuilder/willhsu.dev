Your first keys as a hunter

Things nobody says out loud in the group finder.

Item level before the key

Under about 286 you will be declined. Delves tier 8 and the Renown 9 vendor get you there without a single key.

Mythic 0 first

One run of each of the eight per day, 292 drops, no timer, no affixes. Learn the routes here.

Misdirection on the tank

Shift-Q before every pull. You have no pet to soak threat and Aimed Shot opens hard.

Explosive Shot on two targets

Every pack: E on one mob, E on another. That is most of your AoE.

Interrupt rotation

Counter Shot is 24 seconds. If the group assigns kicks, you take every second cast; if not, kick heals and big AoE casts first.

Stand still for Aimed Shot

It is a 2.5 second cast. Move during Rapid Fire, Steady Shot or on an instant; plan the cast before the swirlie.

Feign Death clears threat and some debuffs

Shift-3. If a boss turns to you, Feign before Turtle.

Lust

You do not have it; the eagle build has no pet. Say so at the start so the group checks who does.

Kith'ix

The Unbinding of Kith'ix is the one-boss raid from 12.1.5. 285 / 298 / 311 / 324 by difficulty, counts for the raid row, and drops Twisted Horror's Tendril, the key trinket.

The tier set

Skulking Viper's Ambush: two-piece makes Explosive Shot last longer and hit harder, four-piece shaves Aimed Shot and Rapid Fire every time it bursts. Pieces are on the gear page with the Catalyst rules.

Valeera Sanguinar

Your companion, tank, healer or damage. Level cap 80 this season. Past 15 she only levels in Bountiful delves, keys not required for that.

Tiers

1 to 11, each unlocks the next. Life counter from tier 4. Tier 3 and up require the campaign done on at least one character.

Keys

Restored Coffer Key = 100 shards, 600 shards a week. Delver's Journey rank 6 lets Naleidea Rivergleam sell two a week.

Weekly

"Purging the Vaults" gives a Trovehunter's Bounty map, one Hero item at tier 8 or above per week. Tier 11 adds a Gilded Stash with 5 Myth crests, four a week.
