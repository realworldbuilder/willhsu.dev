Beast Mastery at 35

How to play a level 35 BM hunter, alone and in a group. It covers the talents you have, the bars and keys (with macros), the exact button order for each kind of pull, what changes in a dungeon, and which pet to bring. Built on my night elf hunter, checked against Wowhead's Forever guides and Blizzard's hotfixes as of 21 November.

Ten minute setup

Do these once, in order. After this, every fight button is under one hand.

1 · Check your talents

Press N, Beast Mastery tab. At 35 you should have 26 points, all in BM. The list is in the next section. Your 35th-level point goes into Frenzy.

2 · Turn on the bars

Escape, Interface, Action Bars: turn on the bottom-left bar, the bottom-right bar and the right bar. Untick Lock Action Bars while you drag, tick it again after.

3 · Make two macros

Type /macro, New, give it a name, pick the question-mark icon, paste. "Mark & Send" and "Pet Back" are in the Macros section.

4 · Fill the bars

Open the spellbook (P) and drag each spell onto the slot shown on the keyboard below. Start the drag on the middle of the icon. Hover each slot after to check the tooltip name: Arcane Shot and Aimed Shot look alike.

5 · Set the keys

Escape, Key Bindings. Main bar: 1 to 5 and Q E R F G T Z. Bottom-left bar ("Bottom Left Action Button 1 to 12"): the same keys with Shift. Bottom-right buttons 1 to 3: X, C, V. Tick "Character Specific Key Bindings" so your alts keep theirs. Q and E were strafe; you lose that on purpose.

6 · Set up the pet bar

Leave the pet bar on its default Ctrl-1 to Ctrl-0. Put the pet on Defensive. Claw, Bite and Growl on autocast (the gold border; right-click to toggle). In a dungeon, right-click Growl to switch autocast off.

Keybinds

Same key plan as my retail hunter. The number row is your shots in priority order. Q E R are the pull and control keys. G is the big cooldown, F is "something is on me", and T and Z keep you alive. Shift is the same keys on the bottom-left bar, for traps and melee. Ctrl is the pet bar.

Try it: press your keys. Hold Shift or Ctrl to see those bars.

Tap a key for its tooltip.

Bottom-right bar, slots 4 to 12 (click)

Call Pet, Dismiss Pet, Revive Pet, Feed Pet, Tame Beast, Eyes of the Beast, Eagle Eye, Aspect of the Beast, Beast Lore.

Right bar (click)

Tracking (Beasts, Humanoids, Hidden), Hearthstone, food, drink, healing potions, bandages, a stack of pet food.

Why the number row

A fight reads left to right: 1 Serpent Sting once, then 2 and 4 over and over (hawk, Aimed Shot), with 3 (Arcane) every third time in place of 2. 5 (Multi-Shot) is only for packs.

Why the letters

Q starts every pull. E (Intimidation) right after. R slows, F gets a mob off you. G is Rapid Fire. T and Z are the far keys so you never press them by accident.

If you used my earlier bar prompt: it put Auto Shot on 6, which makes 13 keys for a 12-slot bar. Drop Auto Shot: every shot starts it anyway, and Blizzard added a setting (16 Oct) that switches between Auto Attack and Auto Shot for you.

The hawk rhythm

Everything about BM damage at 35 is two cooldown pairs. Summon Hawk and Arcane Shot share one 6 second cooldown. Aimed Shot and Multi-Shot share another. So every 6 seconds you get one press from each pair.

Every 18 seconds, on one target

Why hawk, hawk, Arcane

A hawk lasts 18 seconds and only two can be out. Hawk at 0 s, hawk at 6 s, and at 12 s both are still up, so Arcane Shot gets that slot. At 18 s the first hawk leaves and you summon it again.

Why not Multi-Shot

On one mob it uses up Aimed Shot's cooldown for less damage. Only press it on three mobs.

Stand still

Auto Shot needs you to stand still for half a second before it fires (a Forever change, 16 Oct). Every step costs you shots. Move, plant your feet, shoot.

Don't stand too close

Under 8 yards (the dead zone) no ranged attack fires. If a mob is on you, step back first.

Solo

The pet tanks, you shoot. Let it land a couple of hits before you open up, so the mob stays on it.

A normal quest mob

The priority, every fight

  1. Always first. It also sends the pet.
  2. Keep it up on anything that lives longer than about 10 seconds.
  3. On cooldown, until two are out.
  4. Only in the third slot, when both hawks are up.
  5. Whenever it is ready.
  6. Elites and any fight going long.

Three or more mobs

  1. Mark the one the pet should hold.
  2. Hits three. Uses the Aimed Shot cooldown, which is fine here.
  3. Drop it where the pack stands. Pricey, so only on 3 or more.
  4. Instead of Explosive if they are coming at you: slows the pack, you kite.
  5. Keep the hawks on the pet's target.
Mobs that die fast

Save mana: Mark & Send, let Auto Shot work, one Arcane Shot or Aimed Shot. No Serpent Sting.

An elite or a hard mob

Full mana first. Both hawks before it reaches the pet. Intimidation as it arrives, then Rapid Fire, Serpent Sting, and the full rhythm. Scorpid Sting instead of Serpent Sting if it hits the pet hard.

Two mobs

Freezing Trap at your feet, then shoot the second mob so it walks into the trap. The pet kills the first. Traps work in combat in Forever; they all share one 30 sec cooldown. A second beast? Scare Beast it.

A runner

Concussive Shot (R) or Intimidation (E). A runner brings friends.

Something is on you

Wing Clip (F), walk back past 8 yards, keep shooting. After a dodge, Mongoose Bite (Shift-E) lights up. After a parry, Counterattack (Shift-R) roots it.

The pull went wrong

Feign Death (Z). Everything drops. Get up, eat, try again.

Between pulls

Mend Pet (T) before the next pull, not during it. Feed the pet so it stays happy (green). An unhappy pet does less damage. Aspect of the Cheetah (C) to run, back to Hawk (X) before you shoot.

In a group

The tank holds the mobs, your pet joins in, you do damage from range. Your extra jobs: one Freezing Trap per pull, peeling mobs off the healer, and never pulling threat.

Before the first pull

Pet on Defensive (Ctrl-9), never Aggressive: it runs into the next pack. Right-click Growl on the pet bar so autocast is off, or the pet steals the tank's mobs. Aspect of the Hawk on. Aspect of the Pack (at 40) dazes anyone who gets hit, so never run it in a group.

Your trap (the classic trap pull)

Ask which mob is yours to trap (usually the moon). Before the pull, put Freezing Trap down between you and the pack. When the tank pulls, hit your mob with Distracting Shot (Shift-5) or Concussive Shot. It runs to you and freezes. Then stay off it: any damage breaks the ice.

Wait for the tank

Count two or three seconds after the tank's first hit. Then Hunter's Mark on the skull and the rhythm. Rapid Fire on bosses.

Pet on the skull only

Mark & Send sends the pet at your target, so only press it on the kill target. Pet Back if it wanders.

A boss, after the tank has it

Don't

  1. Near a frozen or sheeped mob. It hits three and breaks crowd control.
  2. Same: only when nothing nearby is crowd controlled.
  3. On trash that dies in seconds. Save the mana; drink when the healer drinks.

Fixing things

  1. You are close to pulling aggro: it lowers your threat.
  2. A mob turned to you anyway: drop all your threat.
  3. A mob is on the healer: pull it to you, then Feign Death.
  4. A caster or a runner: the pet stuns it for 3 sec.
  5. Something is on you and the tank is busy: slow it and walk back to the tank.

Hunter's Mark is ranged attack power, so it only helps you (and other hunters), not the melee. Still put it on the skull first: it is your damage.

Your pet

Most of your damage. A cat or a raptor does the most, because both hit 10% harder than other pets and have Claw. A carrion bird does less but is safer, because its Screech weakens what it fights.

At 35 it should have

Claw rank 5, Bite rank 5, Growl rank 4, and Dash if it is a cat. All rank 5s come at pet level 32. Rank 6 Claw and Bite come at 40.

Learning a new rank

Stable your pet. Tame a beast that knows the rank (you can't tame anything above your level in Forever). Use the ability in fights until chat says you learned it. Get your pet back and teach it the rank with Beast Training in your spellbook.

Keep it happy

Feed it the food its family eats. It loses happiness when it dies or you dismiss it, and an unhappy pet does less damage. Green face on the pet frame = full damage.

What it gets from you

The pet copies your crit chance (all of it), 10% of your attack power and 30% of your armor, and 2 health for each point of your Stamina. Agility gear makes the pet better too.

Five mistakes

Arcane Shot on cooldown

It eats the hawk's cooldown. You lose a hawk each time.

Multi-Shot on one mob

It eats Aimed Shot, your biggest hit.

Shooting on the move

Auto Shot waits half a second after you stop. Fewer steps, more shots.

Feign Death and walking off

Forever's tooltip means it: after 6 minutes you actually die. Stand up.

Growl on in a dungeon

The pet pulls mobs off the tank. Autocast off before the first pull.
